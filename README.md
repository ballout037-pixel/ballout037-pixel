## How I Approach Automation

My focus is not simply on making a workflow run. I aim to build solutions that are understandable, testable, and useful to the business.

- **Business-first thinking:** Understand the process and its requirements before automating it.
- **Data reliability:** Validate inputs, identify exceptions, and make outputs easier to review.
- **Practical AI:** Use language models where they add value, with evidence checks and clear limitations.
- **Maintainability:** Document workflows, test expected behavior, and make business rules understandable.
